Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Vyntra Connect ("Vyntra", "we", "us") is a communication platform created by Sahaan Kesavan in Sydney, Australia. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Vyntra Connect, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a display name you choose. Your password is stored as a one-way hash and is never readable by anyone, including us.

Profile information

You may add a profile photo, bio, and other optional details to your profile. This information is visible to other users according to your privacy settings.

Message content

We store the messages you send so they can be delivered to the people and communities you are talking to. Message content is only visible to participants in that conversation.

Usage and device information

We automatically collect basic technical information such as device type, browser version, and approximate region. This helps us keep the service stable and secure.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To deliver messages to the correct people and communities.
  • To show your profile and activity to others according to your settings.
  • To detect abuse, spam, and security issues.
  • To improve features, performance, and accessibility.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not share your messages outside of the conversations they belong to.
  • We do not display environment variables, database records, or deployment configuration details publicly.

4. Data Sharing

We may share limited information with service providers that help us run Vyntra Connect (for example, hosting and authentication providers), only as needed to operate the service. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Vyntra Connect, our users, or the public.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account at any time, after which we will remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to keep it by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data storage and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep Vyntra Connect safe.

8. Children's Privacy

Vyntra Connect is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Vyntra Connect after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Vyntra Connect is created and maintained by Sahaan Kesavan in Sydney, Australia. If you have questions about this privacy policy or your personal information, please reach out through the contact details provided in the app.
