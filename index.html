About Vyntra Connect

Vyntra Connect is a modern communication platform created by Sahaan Kesavan in Sydney, Australia. It is designed to help users communicate through communities and direct messaging in a simple, organised, and user-friendly environment.

The project focuses on making online communication feel accessible, responsive, and easy to navigate across different devices.
